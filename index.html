Process 03 · Steps

How we ship

Step · 01

We find the story

We dig into your brand and ask until the brief confesses: what you’re really selling, who needs to feel it, and the one metric that matters. Agreed before anyone opens a timeline.

Step · 02

We shape it in the open

Every Friday you get a cut you can watch or a page you can click, never a deck about progress. The work stays visible and honest the whole way through.

Step · 03

We ship it, then stay

Release, watch, refine. We stay through the first real audience and the first real campaigns. The release is the opening scene, not the credits.

FAQ

A few honest answers before you decide to reach out.

Got more questions? Talk to Uzair.

Write to Uzair ↗
Who will actually be working on our project?

Uzair leads every project from strategy to final delivery, with our own crew on camera, in the edit and on the design. The people you meet on the first call are the people doing the work. No account managers, no handoffs.

Do you only make films?

No. Film is where we started and it is still the heart of the studio, but the same team designs the brand around it, animates it and builds the site it lands on. One studio, one standard, from the first frame to the last page.

What does working together look like?

We lock scope and the one metric that matters, then work in weekly cuts: something you can watch or click every Friday. Decisions happen on calls, everything else moves async. After release we stay through the first real audience.

How long does a project take?

A campaign film can turn around in days. A full website usually runs three to six weeks. Bigger productions run on a written schedule, with a cut you can watch every Friday. We set dates on day one and keep them.

What do you need from us to start?

One honest conversation about what you’re building and why now. From there you get a written scope, a price and a start date, usually within days. Onboarding is deliberately light so the work starts fast.

What does it cost?

Every project is quoted fixed, so you know the number before we start. A music video and a brand identity are different animals, so we price the work, not the hours. Retainers are there for teams that want us around.
