What we actually do

Six ways a physical event becomes a decision

01

Device firmware & connectivity

Sensors and gateways talking over MQTT or BLE, tuned for the power budget of a device that can't be recharged daily.

02

Real-time monitoring dashboards

A live view of every connected device, built for someone glancing at it between other jobs, not a data analyst.

03

Alerting & escalation logic

Thresholds and escalation paths that wake the right person for the right event, and stay quiet for everything else.

04

Edge-to-cloud data pipelines

Telemetry from thousands of devices ingested, buffered and stored without losing a reading when connectivity drops.

05

Device provisioning & fleet management

Onboarding, firmware updates and remote configuration for a fleet that's grown past the point of doing it by hand.

06

Integration with existing hardware

Working inside the constraints of hardware that's already shipped, not redesigning it from scratch.

How it ships

Discover, build, ship

Discover

Discover

We map the physical environment: what the device sees, how it connects, and what "urgent" actually means for the person receiving an alert.

Build

Build

Firmware, gateway and dashboard built together, tested against the same network conditions the devices will actually sit in.

Ship

Ship

Fleet rollout, monitoring handover and a documented escalation path, agreed before the first unit leaves the bench.

The stack

MQTT and BLE for device connectivity, Node or Go for gateway and backend services, time-series storage (Postgres with TimescaleDB, or InfluxDB), WebSockets for live dashboards.
